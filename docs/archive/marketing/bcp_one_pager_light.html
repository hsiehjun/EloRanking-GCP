Bridging Players & BCP for Greater Engagement & Revenue

A non-competing engagement companion designed to accelerate BCP player list-view subscriptions and TO tournament adoption.
Executive Summary: Partnership Synergy
Best Coast Pairings (BCP) is the premier authority for tabletop tournament registration, live Swiss pairings, and official roster submissions, monetizing through Player Subscriptions (viewing army lists) and Organizer (TO) Subscriptions. OmniTactica is purpose-built as an engagement companion that enhances both revenue streams. By utilizing official BCP match results via a read-only backend sync, OmniTactica gives players an Elo progression system, opponent scouting, and event discovery—creating a direct, daily funnel that drives players to subscribe to BCP to view lists and register for tournaments.
🏆 How OmniTactica Helps Tabletop Players
1. Standardized Elo & Personal Growth: Chess-grade ratings and tier milestones (Apprentice to Everchosen) giving permanent meaning to every BCP match result.
2. Opponent Stats & Matchup Scouting: Instant visibility into opponent win rates, top factions, and head-to-head records before round 1 to ease match anxiety.
3. Event & Community Discovery: Interactive local tournament and game store directory helping competitors find and enter nearby events.
📈 How This Directly Multiplies BCP Revenue
1. Direct List-View Funnel (Player Subs): Scouting an opponent’s stats triggers immediate tactical curiosity. OmniTactica provides direct CTAs: "View Full Roster on BCP", converting players into paying BCP subscribers.
2. Fills Tournament Brackets (TO Subs): Direct "Register & Buy Ticket on BCP" links fill event capacity faster, directly increasing TO retention and tier upgrades.
3. Zero Operational Burden: 100% read-only backend sync. Zero client-side writes. All compute runs on OmniTactica’s cloud infrastructure with zero load on BCP.
Platform Feature Showcase (Live OmniTactica Views) Real-time desktop data views driving engagement back to BCP
1. Player Hub & Career Progression: Standardized Elo rating (1845.5), High Warlord progress bar, verified BCP connection, and match history.
2. Tactical Match Predictor: Head-to-head win probability (Innes Wilson vs. Folger Pyles), expected Elo stakes (+16 / -16), and upset analytics.
3. Matchup Matrix & Nemeses: Highlights Favorite Prey (+56.2 vs. Orks) and Nemeses (-31.0 vs. Aeldari) with head-to-head records across tournament play.
4. Event Hub & BCP Ticketing: Upcoming tournament directory with field Elo ratings, live pairings, and direct "Buy Ticket" / "BCP" links filling brackets.
🛡️ Operational Demarcation: Pure Collaboration, Zero Competition

Tournament Authority

BCP retains 100% authority. OmniTactica has zero pairing or tournament management tools.

List Paywall Protection

OmniTactica never hosts paywalled lists. All roster inspection deep-links directly to BCP's subscription tier.

Read-Only Security

Zero client-side writes. Match records are ingested strictly via backend batch sync with official BCP attribution.
