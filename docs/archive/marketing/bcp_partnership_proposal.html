Executive Summary: What OmniTactica Is & How It Helps BCP

Best Coast Pairings (BCP) is the industry-leading authority for tournament pairing, timing, and official roster collection. BCP generates its revenue primarily through two subscription offerings:

1. Player Subscriptions
Subscribers gain the ability to view opponent and tournament army lists/rosters.
2. Organizer (TO) Subscriptions
Tournament directors pay subscriptions to create, manage, and ticket events on BCP.

OmniTactica is an engagement and progression layer that enhances these two revenue streams. It is not a tournament management system and does not execute pairings. Instead, it utilizes official BCP tournament match data to provide players with what they love most: tracking their personal growth, scouting upcoming opponents, and discovering local competition.

By answering players' questions about their opponents' skill and army records, OmniTactica creates an immediate motivation for players to unlock and inspect the opponent's exact army roster on BCP. Simultaneously, OmniTactica's event discovery engine directs players to register and purchase tickets for upcoming BCP tournaments.

How OmniTactica Helps Tabletop Players

OmniTactica satisfies the core competitive desires of tabletop players, elevating their tournament experience before, during, and after every event:

🏆
Personal Skill Progression & Elo Ratings
Players take immense pride in their competitive journey. OmniTactica calculates a standardized, chess-grade Elo rating (Apprentice to Everchosen) from official BCP matches. Players can celebrate milestone promotions, track their rating trajectory over time, and see tangible reward for their tournament efforts.
📊
Opponent Stats & Matchup Intelligence
Players love knowing who they are playing against. OmniTactica provides instant visibility into opponent win rates, faction mastery, and head-to-head matchup history. It highlights favorite prey factions and toughest nemeses, helping competitors prepare strategically for their games.
📍
Event Discovery & Community Search
Players want to find more places to play. OmniTactica features a built-in event discovery hub and local game store directory that helps competitors find upcoming RTTs, GTs, and local gaming clubs—complete with direct registration links.

Technical Architecture & Security Principles

A lightweight, secure integration that imposes zero operational burden on BCP:

🔒 Zero Client-Side Writes

The client UI has zero write access to BCP or our database. All tournament ingestion is handled strictly via secure, read-only backend sync jobs.

⚡ Zero Server Burden on BCP

All Elo calculations, matchup matrices, and search queries run on OmniTactica's serverless Google Cloud infrastructure.

🏷️ Co-Branding & Attribution

Player profiles and tournament cards feature "Verified Match Data powered by Best Coast Pairings" with direct links back to BCP.

🔗 Direct Roster Links

List inspection buttons route directly to BCP, respecting BCP's subscription paywall for viewing full army lists.
