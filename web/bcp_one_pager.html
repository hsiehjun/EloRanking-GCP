Executive Summary: Partnership Synergy
Best Coast Pairings (BCP) is the premier authority for tabletop event registration, live Swiss pairings, and official roster submissions. BCP monetizes strictly through Player Subscriptions (viewing army lists) and Organizer (TO) Subscriptions. OmniTactica is purpose-built as an engagement companion that enhances both revenue streams. By utilizing official BCP match results via a read-only backend sync, OmniTactica gives players an Elo progression system, opponent scouting, and event discovery—creating a direct, daily funnel that drives players to subscribe to BCP to view lists and register for tournaments.
🏆 How OmniTactica Helps Tabletop Players
1. Standardized Elo & Personal Growth: Chess-grade ratings and tier milestones (Apprentice to Everchosen) giving permanent meaning to every BCP match result.
2. Opponent Stats & Scouting: Instant visibility into opponent win rates, faction mastery, and head-to-head records before round 1 to ease match anxiety.
3. Event & Community Discovery: Interactive local tournament and game store directory helping competitors find and enter nearby events.
📈 How This Directly Multiplies BCP Revenue
1. Direct List-View Funnel (Player Subs): Scouting an opponent’s stats triggers immediate tactical curiosity. OmniTactica provides direct CTAs: "View Full Roster on BCP", converting players into paying BCP subscribers.
2. Fills Tournament Brackets (TO Subs): Direct "Register & Buy Ticket on BCP" links fill event capacity faster, directly increasing TO retention and tier upgrades.
3. Zero Operational Burden: 100% read-only backend sync. Zero client-side writes. All compute runs on OmniTactica’s cloud infrastructure with zero load on BCP.
Player Hub & Elo Progression: Tracks personal rating (1845.5), High Warlord tier progress, verified BCP integration, and tournament history.
Opponent Scouting (Folger Pyles, 90.1% WR): Scouting rival stats directly triggers the player's urge to inspect the exact army list on BCP.
🛡️ Collaboration, Not Competition: Operational Demarcation

Tournament Authority

BCP retains 100% authority. OmniTactica has zero pairing or tournament management tools.

List Paywall Protection

OmniTactica never hosts paywalled lists. All roster inspection deep-links directly to BCP's subscription tier.

Read-Only Security

Zero client-side writes. Match records are ingested strictly via backend batch sync with official BCP attribution.
