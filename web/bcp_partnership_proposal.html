Executive Summary: The Mutual Opportunity

Best Coast Pairings (BCP) is the undeniable gold standard for tournament software and live table pairings. However, BCP's monetization—rooted strictly in Player Subscriptions (to view army lists) and Event Organizer (TO) Subscriptions—experiences friction and seasonality when tournaments conclude on Sunday evening.

OmniTactica provides the persistent, year-round gamification and scouting layer that players crave between events: tracking personal skill progression (chess-grade Elo ratings and tier milestones), scouting upcoming round opponents' historical win rates, and discovering local community events.

By partnering to utilize official BCP tournament match results via a secure, read-only sync, OmniTactica acts as an organic conversion engine for BCP: every time a player scouts an opponent's stats, OmniTactica directs them to BCP to view that opponent's army roster; every time a player seeks to climb the Elo ladder, OmniTactica drives them to register and buy tickets for BCP-hosted tournaments.

🎯
1. Player Progression & Growth
The Need: Players want to know how they stack up globally, track improvement across tournament seasons, and celebrate rating milestones.

The Solution: Robust, standardized Elo ratings (Apprentice to Everchosen) giving every game logged on BCP lasting prestige and competitive purpose.
🔍
2. Opponent Scouting & Intel
The Need: Before round 1, players want to inspect their opponent's faction win rate, playstyle form, and historical matchups.

The Solution: Comprehensive Opponent Matchup Matrices and Head-to-Head records that ease pre-match anxiety and prepare competitors.
🎟️
3. Event & Community Discovery
The Need: Finding local game stores, active clubs, and upcoming regional tournaments is currently fragmented across social media.

The Solution: Integrated Tournament Search and Sparring Radar with direct "Register on BCP" links, filling event tables faster.

Technical Architecture & Security Guarantees

Designed for strict security, minimal overhead, and zero interference with BCP's core operations:

🔒 Zero Client-Side Writes

The client UI has zero write access to tournament records or raw match data. Ingestion is exclusively executed via secure, rate-limited backend cron synchronization.

⚡ Zero Server Burden on BCP

OmniTactica operates on serverless Google Cloud architecture (Cloud Run & Cloud SQL). All heavy computation (Elo deltas, matchup matrices, rankings) is handled off-host.

🏷️ Prominent Co-Branding

Every match result and tournament standings card prominently displays "Verified Match Data powered by Best Coast Pairings" with direct links back to BCP.

🔗 Official Deep-Linking

Roster buttons deliberately route users to BCP's native app or website, strictly honoring BCP's paywall and subscription terms for army list viewing.
