📊 TCG ECONOMY, RARITY DROP CURVE & LIVE-ELO ARCHITECTURE

1. Rarity Tiers & Inverse-Elo Weighting

  • Starter (1450–1550 Elo): Free 28-Faction Pre-Set Deck (No Player Name).
  • Common (1550–1749 Elo): Local RTT Contender (+20 Glory Dup Rebate).
  • Rare (1750–1899 Elo): Regional GT Veteran (+45 Glory Dup Rebate).
  • Epic (1900–2079 Elo): National Major Ace — e.g. Folger P 2070 (+100 Glory Rebate).
  • Mythic Warlord (2080+ Elo): World Icons — Inverse-Elo weighted so 2300+ legends are 3.5× rarer (+250 Glory Rebate).

2. Real Player × Faction Cards (Dynamic Live Elo)

Every collectible card represents a Real Competitive Player + Faction pairing. A single player can have multiple cards across factions — e.g. Folger P (Adeptus Custodes) and Folger P (Necrons) are 2 distinct cards. Because cards link directly to player_ratings, whenever that player's real tournament Elo rises or falls, all of their faction cards update live!

3. Pack Pricing Ladder & Casual vs Tournament

Recruit Pack (150 Glory, 3 Cards) is the impulse entry (~1 RTT win). Tactical Veteran Pack (380 Glory, 5 Cards) acts as the 15%-off mid-tier anchor. Warlord Command Crate (850 Glory, 5 Cards) eliminates Commons (0%) and guarantees an Epic (1900+) or Mythic (2080+) on Slot 1 with +3 Pity! TCG Decks are strictly for Casual Minigame play; Official Tournament mode always uses the real team roster.

🌟 MULTI-FACTION REAL PLAYER SPOTLIGHT (DYNAMIC LIVE ELO SYNC)

One real player can have multiple collectible cards across different factions (e.g. Folger P on Custodes & Necrons). All faction cards of a player dynamically mirror their live Elo!
